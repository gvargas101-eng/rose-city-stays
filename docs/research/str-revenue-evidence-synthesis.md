# Rose City Stays: STR Revenue-Management Evidence Synthesis

**Portfolio:** 11 homes, Tyler, Texas  
**Purpose:** Convert seven independently researched operating memos into one evidence-led decision framework.  
**Evidence boundary:** This synthesis uses **only** the supplied memos and their cited URLs. It adds no new research, no Tyler performance estimate, and no performance guarantee. Source links are retained exactly in [References](#references).  
**Primary decision standard:** Optimize **realized net contribution per available sellable home-night** (with service and compliance guardrails), not occupancy, gross ADR, or a portfolio average alone.

> **How to read this report.** “Evidence” means a source-supported operating principle or externally documented fact. “Hypothesis/test” means a sensible action that still requires Rose City Stays (RCS) data. “Control” means a configuration, financial, or workflow safeguard. No test result should be called causal without a suitable matched comparison and enough completed/cancelled booking maturity.

---

## Executive synthesis

Rose City Stays should first make its 11 homes **measurable and controllable at property-date-stay-pattern level**. The strongest cross-memo evidence is not a citywide price target; it is the operating system: one reservation-level data dictionary, daily on-books (OTB) snapshots, an economic floor per home and length-of-stay (LOS) pattern, a documented pricing authority, all-in guest-facing parity checks, and short, reversible tests measured on cancellation-adjusted net contribution.

Tyler has plausible, source-documented demand inputs—healthcare, higher education, employers, seasonal civic events, and recurring Horse Park dates—but the supplied evidence does **not** establish neighborhood demand, portfolio occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, event compression, or a local rate premium. AirDNA’s public page is a third-party modeled-market input and may be used only as directional context, not as an RCS target or forecast. Confirmed events should therefore trigger monitoring and testable restrictions/pricing decisions, never an automatic surcharge.

The immediate commercial opportunity is **revenue protection**: reduce pricing/restriction synchronization errors, prevent avoidable host cancellations, stop unprofitable discounts or one-night stays, reconcile payouts and taxes, and make direct/OTA checkout totals comparable. The next opportunity is **controlled yield experimentation**: booking-curve actions, gap release, LOS fences, rate/fee presentation, and qualified direct conversion improvements. Longer-term opportunity is a repeatable portfolio decision system that distinguishes unique home positioning from policy experiments across genuinely substitutable homes.

## 2. Reconciled policy positions and claims not carried forward

The source memos are largely complementary. The following table resolves their boundaries so RCS does not turn useful principles into unsupported rules.

| Topic | Apparent tension | Reconciled position | Claims discarded or deferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tyler events | Specific event dates coexist with no local compression evidence. | Event dates are **watch periods**; apply small reversible price/restriction changes only after RCS pickup, inquiries, availability, lead time, and LOS support them. | An event automatically warrants a premium; a city event benefits every home. |
| AirDNA | A modeled seasonality score is available but portfolio data are absent. | Use as directional third-party context only; RCS OTB and realized results govern. | The 97/100 score is an RCS demand forecast or target. |
| Pricing source of truth | PriceLabs controls, Hostaway functions, and OTA settings can all affect guest-facing price. | Appoint **one documented pricing authority and propagation map** for each home/channel. PriceLabs may propose/apply rules; Hostaway may distribute; the guest-facing OTA/website outcome is the final verification point. | “Hostaway must be the direct-rate master” before cost cards and precedence are documented. |
| Base price versus dynamic price | Base price is described as an annual average while demand requires dynamic changes. | The base is a stable home-specific anchor; dynamic adjustments operate around it. Rebase only after persistent evidence. | A base price is a floor, peak, or a portfolio-wide uniform value. |
| Floors/minimum prices | Tool default safety limits exist, while economic floors are required. | RCS’s floor is a contribution calculation by home, channel, and LOS; tool limits are implementation guardrails only. | Vendor default minimum is a viable economic minimum. |
| MinLOS controls | Controls can improve sequence economics but restrict shopper eligibility. | Default rules are hypotheses; every temporary control must pass contribution, capacity, and searchability checks and have expiry. | Blanket 2-night/weekend/CTA rules as proven revenue optimization. |
| Discounts | Specific discount percentages were proposed alongside caveats against unverified optimal discounts. | 5% early/8–14 day and 10% ≤7 day are optional **pre-registered pilot arms**, non-stacking and floor-bounded. | Those levels are “right” for Tyler or will increase revenue. |
| Net revenue and contribution | Memos define net revenue and caution that RevPAR/RevPAN is not profit. | Maintain two distinct measures: **net lodging revenue** (after channel/payment/refund effects, excluding taxes and nonretained pass-throughs) and **net contribution** (net lodging revenue less variable stay/turnover/service costs). | Calling net RevPAN “profit,” or mixing tax/cleaning pass-through with revenue. |
| Direct booking | Direct economics may be better, but OTA diversion is prohibited and direct fees can add friction. | Improve direct conversion only for direct-owned traffic; measure all-in checkout completion and cancellation-adjusted contribution. | Moving OTA inquiries/rebookings/extensions/payment off-platform; claiming direct is always more profitable. |
| Fee/ancillary strategy | Fees may produce revenue but may harm conversion or delivery quality. | Add or change one disclosed fee/service at a time and measure contribution plus funnel/review damage. | Gross fee collections or refundable deposits are profit. |

### Explicitly excluded until RCS produces evidence

1. Any Tyler occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, lead-time, event-attendance, event-compression, competitor-rate, or conversion number not present in RCS controlled data.
2. Any claim that a health-care, university, corporate, festival, fair, airshow, or equestrian segment causes a booking or rate premium.
3. A universal fee, discount, MinLOS, cancellation policy, base-price change, markup, or commission assumption.
4. Combining native Airbnb and Booking.com conversion/cancellation metrics without preserving each platform’s denominator and definition.
5. Medical or other sensitive-attribute inference from guests, messages, or travel patterns.
6. Causal conclusions from a single property, a raw before/after ADR change, or occupancy alone.

## 3. Measurement contract and evidence that RCS must create

### 3.1 Canonical definitions

Use **stay date** for realized revenue/occupancy and **as-of date** for OTB/pickup. Retain daily OTB snapshots; otherwise historic pace cannot be reconstructed reliably.

| Measure | Definition / formula | Required control |
|---|---|---|
| Sellable available home-nights | Calendar nights less documented owner, maintenance, legal, and unavailable blocks. | Preserve block reason and timestamp; never count blocked inventory as failed demand. |
| Occupancy | Occupied stayed nights ÷ sellable available nights × 100. [R49] | Report by home, cohort, channel, stay type, and portfolio; separate cancelled/no-show conventions. |
| Gross ADR | Gross accommodation revenue for stayed paid nights ÷ occupied stayed nights. [R50] | Keep taxes, refundable deposits, and non-accommodation pass-throughs out of the numerator. |
| Gross RevPAN / RevPAR | Gross accommodation revenue ÷ sellable available home-nights; equivalently ADR × occupancy. [R51] | “Home-night” is the appropriate unit; do not obscure property heterogeneity. |
| Realized net lodging revenue | Realized accommodation revenue + retained ancillary revenue − commissions − payment costs − refunds/chargebacks − channel costs. | Exclude taxes and nonretained pass-through cleaning/fees; retain a line-item audit trail. |
| Net ADR / net RevPAN | Realized net lodging revenue ÷ occupied stayed nights / sellable available home-nights. | Label clearly as an internal management metric; it is not profit. |
| Net contribution / ContribPAN | Net lodging revenue − variable cleaning/turnover labor, supplies/laundry, incremental utilities, pet/extra-guest/early-late delivery cost, service recovery, and other variable stay costs; divided by available nights for ContribPAN. | Agree cost allocation rules and retain actual vendor/cleaner invoices. Fixed costs/NOI remain separate. |
| Pickup | Net new booked/extension nights and revenue since prior snapshot, less cancellations/shortenings. [R52] | Compare like DTA, weekday, season, home/cohort, and event status; report gross and net pickup. |
| Pace index | Current OTB at a given days-to-arrival (DTA) ÷ matched baseline OTB at same DTA × 100. | Baseline should use matched weekday/season/home/event status rather than same-date-last-year alone. |
| Lead time | Check-in date − booking-confirmed date. | Store booking confirmation timestamp and modification/cancellation history. |
| LOS / ALOS | Occupied stayed nights per completed reservation. | Separate 1, 2, 3–6, 7–13, 14–27, and 28+ night bands. |
| Cancellation rate | Cancelled reservations ÷ confirmed reservations; also cancelled accommodation value ÷ confirmed accommodation value. | Preserve native OTA denominator; do not blend unlike reporting definitions. |
| Direct conversion | Completed direct bookings ÷ eligible direct listing/search sessions. | Define “eligible,” cross-domain booking tracking, and session attribution in GA4 before reporting. |
| Restriction health | Orphan gaps, nights unavailable by reason, guest-search eligibility, sync exceptions, floor/ceiling hits, and overrides. | Audit at the guest-facing channel/site—not only in internal tools. |

### 3.3 Data-quality gates before targets or experiments

1. **ID map:** A stable property ID, Hostaway listing ID, PriceLabs listing ID, direct-site URL, Stripe metadata/reference, and each OTA listing ID for all 11 homes.
2. **Block taxonomy:** Owner, maintenance, turnover hold, legal/operational closure, manual hold, and unknown. Unknown blocks are data defects.
3. **Gross-to-net reconciliation:** Monthly sample reconciliation of reservation line items to Airbnb/Vrbo/Booking.com/Stripe payout and refund data, including taxes and fee treatment.
4. **Guest-facing QA:** After rule or bulk updates, check one current and one future date per channel for availability, total price, fees, policy, and MinLOS. Maintain evidence/screenshots or a check log.
5. **Cancellation maturity:** Do not score a treatment at booking creation alone. Review when its relevant arrival/cancellation window has matured and document rebooked cancelled nights.
6. **Segment discipline:** Purpose tags are voluntary, operationally relevant, and non-sensitive; “unknown” is valid. Do not derive medical/health attributes.
7. **Denominator discipline:** Retain channel-native denominators for conversion and cancellation; no blended dashboard rate without a documented harmonization method.

## 4. Prioritized actions by impact horizon

> **Impact horizon means when a lever can credibly begin to affect revenue protection or decision quality—not a forecasted dollar lift.** Every change remains subject to host/channel policy, tax, legal, HOA, accessibility, privacy, and contractual review.

## 6. Compliance and control boundaries

- **Tax:** The supplied Tyler HOT source and memo state that stays of 30 days or fewer are transient and cite a 7% City HOT plus an additional 2%; this is a control prompt, **not** a determination of RCS liability, exemption, remittance, or 30+ day treatment. Reconcile reservation tax collection/remittance/exemptions and written 30+ day agreements monthly, using current legal/tax advice and channel configuration. [R12]
- **Platform rules:** Keep OTA-originated booking, payment, extensions, and rebooking on that OTA; do not use current/future/repeat OTA communications to divert bookings off-platform. [R38, R42]
- **Sensitive data:** A caregiver/medical-related purpose label may be offered voluntarily for service/operational analysis; no sensitive condition or diagnosis should be inferred or stored for revenue management.
- **Accessibility, animal, deposit, and damage processes:** Confirm current channel rules and applicable law before charges, deposits, claims, or animal-related fees. Refundable deposits remain liabilities, not revenue. [R57, R60, R63, R64]
- **Portfolio test integrity:** Do not proclaim a successful intervention based on one booking, a gross-ADR increase, or a raw before/after comparison. Account for internal switching among homes, displacement of future demand, cancellations, and operational outcomes.
