# In-Property Direct-Booking & Review Plan

**Prepared for Rose City Stays**  
**Primary goal:** Turn satisfied in-house guests into returning direct-booking guests without adding friction or making claims that are hard to keep.

## The core sign

The supplied A5 sign is designed for a tabletop acrylic holder, frame, or wall plaque. Its QR code opens the direct-booking website and is tagged for **in-property / return-guest** tracking in Google Analytics. Property-specific PDF versions use a different `utm_content` value, so you can see which homes produce the most scans and return-booking demand.

The sign says: “Love this stay? Come back direct.” That is an invitation, not a price promise. It works for leisure, family, corporate, and medical guests and keeps the decision focused on the next stay rather than disrupting the guest’s current experience.

## Placement that will get used

Use the sign in one primary location per home: on the kitchen counter or coffee bar, next to the Wi-Fi card, guest book, or local recommendations. This is a natural pause point where people already have their phones in hand. Use a small 5×7-inch tabletop frame or acrylic holder so the QR code stays clean, upright, and easy to scan.

Add a second, smaller touchpoint near the checkout instructions. Do not repeat a large sign in every room. One prominent sign plus one quiet checkout reminder feels intentional rather than pushy.

Before printing all copies, test the sign from about 18 inches away with both an iPhone and Android phone. Keep the white border around the QR code clear; do not put it behind reflective glass, near glossy patterns, or under very dim lighting.

## Review strategy: ask for an honest review, not a five-star review

Do not ask guests for a “five-star review,” filter who receives a request, or offer a discount, gift, upgrade, or contest entry in exchange for a review. Google permits businesses to share a review-request link or QR code, including in a printed display, but requires reviews to reflect genuine experiences and prohibits incentives. Airbnb also prohibits incentives or pressure designed to influence a review. [1] [2]

Use this checkout message instead:

> We hope you loved your Rose City stay. Your honest feedback helps our small local team keep improving. If you have a minute after checkout, we would be grateful for a review.

For an in-house review card, use a separate QR code that goes only to the Google review-request link generated inside your Google Business Profile. Google specifically supports generating and printing that QR code. Do not send guests to a general Google search or ask them to mention particular phrases. [1]

## Three guest moments to use

**During the stay:** Place the direct-booking sign by the Wi-Fi details or coffee station. The call to action is future-facing: “Save this for your next Tyler trip.” Do not request a review at this stage.

**Checkout eve:** Send a Hostaway message that thanks the guest and shares the same direct-booking link. Use it as a convenience message, not a hard sell:

> Thank you again for choosing Rose City Stays. If Tyler brings you back, you can view our full collection and book direct anytime at rosecitystays.com.

**After checkout:** Once the guest has had time to leave, send the honest-review request. Keep the review request separate from discount offers. If you want to offer returning guests a loyalty benefit, mention that in a later campaign or direct-booking message rather than tying it to a review.

## A simple repeat-guest offer

The highest-engagement reason to scan is a clear returning-guest benefit. Use one benefit that you can consistently honor, such as early access to your direct availability, a returning-guest code, or a direct-booking perk when available. Do not print a permanent percentage on the sign until you are ready to honor it across all homes and dates.

A strong optional checkout line is:

> Planning another Tyler visit? Save Rose City Stays for direct access to our full home collection and returning-guest offers.

This keeps flexibility in the offer while giving guests a reason to scan.

## Referral and social proof

Add a small line to the guest book or fridge card:

> Traveling with friends, family, or coworkers next time? Scan to find the right Rose City home for every group.

This is especially useful because a guest may not need another stay personally but may know a traveling nurse, family member, golf group, or work team who does. Your property-specific tracking will show whether these on-site prompts generate traffic.

## Measure it monthly

In GA4, review traffic where `utm_source=in_property`, `utm_medium=qr`, and `utm_campaign=return_guest`. Compare scans, engaged sessions, property-page views, checkout starts, and purchases by `utm_content` to identify the homes and placements that work best. Replace signage only if the data or guest feedback suggests it is not being noticed.

A practical first-month target is simple: distribute the property-specific signs, verify each scan reaches the website, and record the sign placement in a one-line property checklist. After 30 days, move signs only in homes with few scans before changing the message.

## Recommended production

Print on matte heavyweight cardstock, then place in a clear 5×7-inch acrylic tabletop stand. A5 is slightly larger than 5×7 inches; if you prefer a 5×7 frame, print at 95% scale or let the print shop trim it. Matte stock reduces glare and scans better than a glossy finish. Keep the main sign inside the home; do not use it in a way that exposes a QR link to non-guests outside the property.
